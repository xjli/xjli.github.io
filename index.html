Xiujun Li

Multimodal LLMs, LLMs, NLP, Vision and Language

I am currently a Staff Research Scientist at Apple. I earned my PhD from UW CSE with Yejin Choi in 2024. Prior to this, I worked five years at Microsoft Research. My research experience is on Dialog, Deep Reinforcement Learning, NLP, Vision and Language, Multimodal LLMs etc. Recently, I am also interested in Video Generation.
